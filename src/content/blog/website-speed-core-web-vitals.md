Speed is the part of web design most business owners never think about, and the part that quietly costs them the most.

A slow website loses visitors before they read a single word. And Google measures how your site performs for real users, which means a slow site can hold back your rankings too.

## What are Core Web Vitals?

Core Web Vitals are a set of metrics Google uses to measure real-world user experience. The three main ones:

| Metric | What it measures | Good score |
|---|---|---|
| **LCP** (Largest Contentful Paint) | How fast the main content shows up | Under 2.5 seconds |
| **INP** (Interaction to Next Paint) | How quickly the page responds when you tap or click | Under 200 milliseconds |
| **CLS** (Cumulative Layout Shift) | How much the page jumps around while loading | Under 0.1 |

In plain English: does it load fast, does it respond fast, and does it stay still?

## Why speed matters for local businesses

**Visitors leave.** People searching for a local service are usually in a hurry. If your site takes too long, they hit back and call the next result.

**Rankings.** Page experience is one of many signals Google considers. Speed alone won't make you #1, but when two businesses are otherwise similar, the better experience has an edge.

**Mobile reality.** Your customers aren't always on fast Wi-Fi. They're on cellular data in a parking lot on Keystone or in a basement with a leaking water heater. Your site needs to work there.

## How to check your speed

Go to **PageSpeed Insights** (search for it on Google), enter your URL, and look at the **mobile** score. Mobile is what matters most, and it's usually lower than desktop.

- **90–100:** Excellent
- **50–89:** Needs work
- **Under 50:** Probably costing you customers

## What makes websites slow

### Oversized images
The most common culprit. A photo straight off a phone can be several megabytes. It should usually be under 200KB and in a modern format like WebP or AVIF.

### Too many plugins and scripts
Every chat widget, slider, tracking pixel, and page-builder feature adds code the browser has to download and run. Many small business sites load several times more JavaScript than they need.

### Cheap shared hosting
Bargain hosting often means your site shares a server with hundreds of others. When they're busy, you're slow.

### Page builders
Drag-and-drop builders are convenient, but they often produce heavy, bloated code.

## Quick wins if you're stuck with your current site

1. Compress every image (free tools like Squoosh work great)
2. Remove plugins and widgets you don't actually use
3. Turn off auto-playing video and image sliders
4. Enable caching (most hosts have a setting or plugin for this)
5. Consider a CDN like Cloudflare
